The Power Law model

The Bitcoin Power Law, developed by physicist Giovanni Santostasi, models BTC price as a power function of time since the genesis block (Jan 3, 2009): log₁₀(price) = 5.82 × log₁₀(days) + B.

This calculator anchors B to your current price input, creating a forward projection consistent with observed historical growth. The exponent (5.82) reflects the network effect dynamics observed since 2009.

Unlike linear or exponential models, the power law captures the decelerating but sustained growth as Bitcoin matures — making it more conservative than pure exponential assumptions over long time horizons.

The Bridge Strategy

Retiring before 59½ creates a funding gap: traditional accounts (401k, IRA) cannot be accessed without a 10% penalty. Your BTC portfolio must carry the entire load during this bridge period.

The calculator models this by computing two distinct portfolio requirements: (1) bridge fund = annual spending × years until 59½, and (2) perpetual portfolio = annual spending / safe withdrawal rate, reduced by any post-59½ income.

Your total freedom number is the sum. The chart shows whether your projected BTC value at your target age clears this bar.

Reading the Chart

The gold curve is your portfolio — growing with the power law through your accumulation years, then declining as you sell BTC to fund retirement spending. BTC's continued appreciation means the decline is slower than a flat-asset model.

The cream dashed line is your total freedom number. The green dashed line is just the bridge fund portion. The vertical gold line marks your target retirement age; the silver line marks 59½.

Where the gold curve first crosses the cream line before retirement — that's your actual earliest freedom date given the power law trajectory. Adjust your inputs to move that intersection.
