The Power Law model

Unlike CAGR projections that assume constant percentage growth, the Bitcoin Power Law (Santostasi, 2024) models price as a function of time since genesis:

log₁₀(Price) = −17.1 + 5.8 × log₁₀(days since genesis)

This means growth slows over time — aggressively high early, tapering as BTC matures. Far more realistic than assuming a constant 20% forever.

The pre-59½ problem

Early retirement creates a gap: your IRA and 401k are locked until age 59½ without penalties. This "bridge period" must be funded entirely from your BTC portfolio (or other taxable accounts).

  • The longer you retire early, the larger this bridge
  • Your BTC portfolio must sustain full spending alone during bridge years
  • At 59½, retirement accounts kick in — reducing the draw on BTC
  • The "Adjusted Freedom Number" accounts for this relief

Reading the chart

  • Gold line: Your BTC portfolio accumulating via DCA + power law growth
  • Green line: Portfolio after you retire — drawdown rate vs continued BTC growth
  • Dashed gold: Your full Freedom Number (annual spend ÷ SWR)
  • Dashed green: Adjusted Freedom Number — what you actually need if 401k helps post-59½
  • Faint cream: Cumulative living expenses from now — the total cost of your life

If the gold line crosses the dashed line before your target retirement age, you're on track. If the green line stays above zero through life expectancy, the plan is sustainable.
